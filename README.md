Two lines: one everyday thing explained to a 10-year-old through one analogy, and one honest sentence about what that analogy maps and what it leaves out.

> A crosswalk is a bridge made of paint that lets you walk safely over a river of cars.
> It maps the safe crossing, but it leaves out that a real bridge holds you up, the paint only tells drivers where to stop.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes exactly those two lines to `/output/entry.txt`.
See [METHOD.md](METHOD.md) for the 30-candidate tournament that picked them.
